### 1. Skippable In-Stream Ads (`TrueView CPV`)
Plays before or during videos in your exact music genre or content vertical. Because viewers can skip after 5 seconds, your budget is only charged when a real viewer chooses to watch at least **30 seconds** (or the entire video if shorter) or interacts with your call-to-action.
- **Best for:** High-volume music video launches, album releases, and brand awareness.

Displays your video thumbnail and headline directly in **YouTube Search results**, the **YouTube Mobile Home Feed**, and alongside **Related Videos**. Every view begins with a deliberate user click on your thumbnail.
- **Best for:** Maximizing subscriber conversion, high watch-time retention, comments, and long-term organic ranking.
